# AgentSure

> **AI risk quantification + insurance for the AI agent economy.**

We build the testing-to-insurance flywheel for organizations deploying AI agents:

- **Pulse** — Singapore-first AI regulatory intelligence, atomic clause search, and `regulation ↔ insurance peril` crosswalks.
- **Quantify** — AI testing SaaS: red team, hallucination, bias, agent safety, with framework crosswalks (IMDA MGF / MAS Veritas / NIST RMF / ISO 42001).
- **Coverage** — Liability insurance for AI failure modes (agent over-reach, hallucination, data leakage, token loss), powered by Quantify telemetry.

## Why we exist

AI deployers face three intertwined problems:
1. **Compliance** is fragmenting into hundreds of overlapping frameworks
2. **Testing** for AI agents is immature and disconnected from monetary risk
3. **Insurance** for AI failure modes is virtually nonexistent in APAC

AgentSure unifies all three around a shared risk taxonomy and atomic regulatory ontology.
