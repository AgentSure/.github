# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@agentsure.tech** with:

- A description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce
- Affected URL / product / version
- Your contact for follow-up

**Do not** open a public GitHub issue for security reports.

We commit to:

- Acknowledging your report within 2 business days
- Providing a remediation timeline within 7 business days
- Crediting you in our security advisory (with your consent)

## Scope

In scope:
- https://agentsure.tech and all subdomains
- Quantify portal at `*.up.railway.app` / `quantify.agentsure.tech`
- Pulse APIs at `agentsure.tech/api/*`

Out of scope:
- Third-party SaaS we depend on (report directly to them: Vercel, Railway, Supabase, Cloudflare)
- Social engineering / physical attacks
- Denial of service attacks

Reported issues that materially improve our security will be acknowledged here.
